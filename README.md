# Goals FC Club App

A club app for a youth soccer club: club information, coaches, the training schedule, full rosters, and the part most club apps miss, player check ins.

## What it does
- Club tab: club info, key numbers, and announcements
- Training tab: every session with live check in counts (in, maybe, out, not answered)
- Roster tab: every player by squad, with position, jersey number, guardian contact, and coach notes
- Check In tab: players or parents tap in, maybe, or out for each session, so coaches plan around real numbers
- Coaches tab: the coaching staff with roles, squads, and bios
- Insights tab: reliability percentages per player and a nudge list of who has not answered for the next session
- Coach mode: one tap turns on adding and editing for club info, announcements, sessions, players, and coaches

## How it runs
One HTML file in the browser. All data stays on the device, no account, no server. Comes with sample club data that a coach can replace in coach mode in minutes.

## Get it
Open index.html in a browser.
